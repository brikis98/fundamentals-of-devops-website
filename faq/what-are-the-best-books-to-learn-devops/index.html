Practical guidance

  • Choose one primary hands-on book and implement its examples end to end.
  • Supplement with focused references on reliability, security, and architecture.
  • Prioritize resources that teach trade-offs, not only tool syntax.
  • Build a portfolio project while reading so knowledge becomes operational skill.
